Postradamus privacy policy

Postradamus is a private posting tool used by Jason Berlin to publish his own videos and podcast announcements. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive: Postradamus saves the videos you record into folders in your own Google Drive and reads those folders to name new files. It does not read, change or share any other files.
  • Metricool: Postradamus asks Metricool to schedule the posts you create, on the social accounts you have connected there.

What it stores

The approvals for Google Drive and Metricool are stored privately on the app's server so it can keep working. Videos are stored only in your Google Drive. Your recent posting history stays on your own phone.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. Data goes only to Google Drive and Metricool, to do what you asked.

Removing access

You can remove Postradamus's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and its Metricool access in your Metricool settings.
